**Volt meter / Tester** <br>
For measuring voltage and testing wires.

- Maximum voltage 25 V
- Maximum current 255 Ohm

- The voltage or tester is selected by a switch


Resistance is measured approximately. <br>
The main function is to test contacts and wires. <br>
When the resistance is below the threshold (10 or 25), a signal sounds. <br>

*** settings ***  <br>
fuses: <br>
0x7A LOW <br>
0xFF HIGH <br>
All settings are factory defaults <br>
except the 8-digit divider (CKDIV8=1) <br>

R3=620...680 Ohm <br>

Created in Flowcode 5.5.2.1 <br>
